# Elemental identity v0.1

**Name:** عناصر / Elemental. **Tagline:** چرخهٔ جهان / Chronicles of Balance.

**Mark:** one original eight-point compass; its four cardinal tips carry the forces. Each element uses a distinctive geometric sigil rather than stock emoji: water `◈`, fire `✦`, earth `⬡`, wind `≋`. The center dot signifies the world kept in balance.

**Art direction:** cartographer's fantasy atlas × distant planetary observatory. Restrained gold lines over midnight blue, with four material palettes:

- Water: arctic cyan `#84D6DB` over deep ocean `#102C41`.
- Fire: glowing copper `#EFAB84` over oxblood `#3B211F`.
- Earth: lichened green `#A6C5A0` over forest slate `#253A32`.
- Wind: lilac haze `#B8B3DB` over navy-purple `#2C2D4A`.

**Typography:** Vazirmatn for Persian UI and high-contrast headings; fine-letterspaced Latin labels for collectible/card metadata. Real production should vendor appropriately licensed webfonts instead of requiring Google Fonts access.

**Interaction:** tactile card press; compact 15-second loops, consequence dialogs, mobile safe-area nav, reduced motion support, keyboard-visible focus, haptic feedback where Telegram supports it. A public browser opens **clearly labelled practice mode**; no false progress saving.

**Asset policy:** All illustration/mark shapes generated originally for this starter. Legacy icons, logos, stock AI art and old GitHub Pages `docs/assets` are not carried over.
